# Business and validation

## Positioning

“A co-creation studio for creators who want their community to build the show with them.” The paid value would be repeatable interactive formats, saved creations, original module packs and production convenience. Model access alone is not a durable advantage.

## Customer interview questions

Ask about the creator's last actual audience-participation segment. How did they collect ideas? What was difficult? What preparation was required? What did their moderators do? Would they run this specific city scenario next week? What would stop them? Which tool would it replace?

Do not ask only “Would you pay for this?” Ask for a second session, a concrete workflow commitment or a paid pilot after the prototype is useful.

## Validation sequence

Five interviews → three free pilot sessions → observed fixes → repeat sessions → paid-pilot experiment. Measure setup time, creator interventions, rejected/misclassified contributions, participation across revisions and willingness to schedule again.

Suggested initial decision gate: at least two of three pilot hosts request a second session without being pressed. This is a hypothesis for product selection, not evidence already collected.

## Possible offers

| Offer | Proposed experiment |
| --- | --- |
| Free | Local mode, one scenario, standard stage |
| Creator | USD 15–29/month for saved sessions, themes and more original modules |
| Studio | Collaborator workspaces and branded shows, priced after interviews |
| Event | Producer setup and custom scenario licensing |

No payment integration is included. These are unvalidated prices, not forecasts. A hundred subscribers at USD 19 equals USD 1,900 gross/month before costs and taxes; it is arithmetic, not an earnings expectation.

## Cost model

Separate compute/hosting, storage, model inputs/outputs, support and content production. Local deterministic mode has no required provider calls. Embeddings are batched by user request. Generation runs only when asked. Use current provider prices when setting a budget; this document does not quote prices that can change.

## Defensibility to build

An excellent original module grammar, tested play formats, creator-owned asset libraries, clear attribution, reliable live operation and a distribution relationship with repeat creators. Do not collect unnecessary chat history or profile viewers as a shortcut to a moat.
